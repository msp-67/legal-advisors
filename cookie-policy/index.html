Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device and remember information about your visit.

2. Cookies We Use

Strictly necessary storage. This website stores a single entry in your browser's local storage, under the key la-cookie-consent, to remember your cookie choice so the banner is not shown again. It is essential for the website to comply with your choice, holds no personal data and does not require your consent.

Analytics cookies. This website currently uses no analytics and sets no analytics cookies. Should analytics be introduced in the future, they would be activated only after your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 89 of Act No. 127/2005 Sb., and this policy would be updated accordingly.

Third-party content. This website embeds no third-party content. Fonts are self-hosted from this domain, and no external fonts, maps, videos, or scripts are loaded. Technical server logs kept by the hosting provider, Vercel Inc., are described in the Privacy Policy.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You may withdraw or change your consent at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page, or by adjusting your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

4. Changes to This Policy

The current version is always available at legaladvisors.cz/cookie-policy.
