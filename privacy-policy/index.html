5. Your Rights

Under GDPR and Act No. 110/2019 Sb. you have the right to:

  • access your data (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • request erasure, subject to legal retention obligations (Art. 17 GDPR);
  • restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR);
  • withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Important: where data is subject to attorney-client privilege (§ 21 of Act No. 85/1996 Sb.), certain rights — in particular access requests by third parties — may be restricted to protect client interests.

To exercise your rights, write to jan@spacil.attorney. Requests are answered within one month, extendable by two further months for complex requests per Art. 12(3) GDPR.
