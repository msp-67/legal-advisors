About

About Jan Spáčil

I understand that your business, wealth, and family legacy are more than numbers or contracts — they represent your life’s work, your values, and your future. As a highly experienced professional with more than 30 years of practice, I provide personalised legal counsel directly to each client. There is no junior associate between you and your attorney. When you engage me, you have my full attention and personal accountability on every matter. Depending on the complexity and nature of your case, I collaborate with a carefully selected network of experienced legal specialists and expert advisors — each engaged individually and bound by strict professional confidentiality obligations. This approach ensures you receive exactly the expertise your situation demands, with the personal attention of a dedicated attorney. Whether you are structuring assets, planning for future generations, seeking legal clarity in business, or facing challenges relating to your family and its property — I stand by your side as a committed partner in your success.
